# Notes on Mathematics

*A concise introduction to the language of proof.*

Mathematics begins with definitions, but it advances through careful reasoning. These notes collect a few elementary ideas and show how they fit together.

## Reading these notes

Each chapter is organized into short sections. Expand a chapter in the contents to find its pages; the current section is highlighted. The text supports inline mathematics such as $a^2+b^2=c^2$ and displayed equations:

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}.
$$

A proof is an explanation that makes every step clear. It need not be long to be complete.

> The purpose of a proof is not merely to convince, but to explain.
